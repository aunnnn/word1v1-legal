Privacy Policy

Word 1v1 · Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word 1v1 (“the App”), developed by Wirawit Rueopas (“we,” “us”), handles information when you use the App on iPhone or iPad.

Summary

Word 1v1 is a real-time 1v1 Wordle-style word game. It does not require an account of ours. We do not operate our own servers. Stats and settings stay on your device. Multiplayer uses Apple Game Center. There are currently no in-app purchases, and we do not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

Information we process

  • Apple Game Center. If you use Game Center, sign-in, matchmaking (random opponents and friend invites / room codes), real-time play, and leaderboards are provided by Apple GameKit. Real-time match data is sent peer-to-peer between players’ devices via Game Center — for example guess color results and progress, emoji reactions, and your Game Center display name / alias. Scores may be submitted to Apple Game Center leaderboards. We do not operate Game Center and do not receive this traffic on servers of our own. Apple processes Game Center data under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • What opponents can see. During a match, opponents can see your Game Center display name, in-game progress as tile colors (not the letters you typed), and reactions you send.
  • Game Center friends. The App accesses your Game Center friends list only if you grant permission (the iOS friends-list permission). You can refuse or later revoke that permission in iOS Settings.
  • Local network. The App may request local network permission so Game Center can find nearby friends. This stays on the local network; we do not collect it on our own servers.
  • On-device app data. Stats, streaks, and settings (sound, haptics, high-contrast, reduce motion, reactions) are stored locally on your device (UserDefaults). They are not uploaded to us.
  • Share. If you use Share, the App shares only text you choose, through the iOS share sheet. We do not upload that content to our servers.
  • Dictionary. Word lookup uses Apple’s built-in dictionary on your device. We do not send lookup queries to servers of our own.
  • Diagnostics. Apple may collect crash and performance diagnostics if you have opted into sharing analytics with app developers in iOS Settings. We do not operate a separate analytics or advertising SDK.

What we do not do

  • We do not create user accounts or ask for your name, email, or password inside the App.
  • We do not operate our own servers.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not include analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not use App Tracking Transparency / cross-app tracking for advertising.
  • We do not currently offer in-app purchases.

Third parties

  • Apple. App Store, Game Center (GameKit), device OS permissions, the built-in dictionary, the share sheet, and optional developer diagnostics. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Data retention

Stats, streaks, and settings you keep in the App remain on your device until you delete them or delete the App. Match data exchanged during a game is sent between devices via Game Center for that match. Leaderboard scores and Game Center account data are retained by Apple as needed to provide those services.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided such information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps. Game Center parental controls and Screen Time can restrict multiplayer and other Game Center features.

Your choices

  • You can sign out of Game Center or restrict Game Center (including with parental controls and Screen Time) in iOS Settings.
  • You can deny or revoke Game Center friends-list and local network permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete local app data by deleting the App or clearing its data via iOS Settings where available.
  • You can turn off sharing analytics with app developers in iOS Settings.

International users

The App is distributed through the App Store. Game Center and other processing by Apple may occur in the United States or other countries where Apple operates.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
